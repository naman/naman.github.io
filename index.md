> **_NOTE:_**  I am on the Academic Job Market for postdoc/tenure-track positions starting from 2027 in Information, Computer Science, Communications, and related interdisciplinary fields.


Namaste! I am Naman (he/him), a Computer Science PhD Candidate at the [University of Wisconsin-Madison, USA](https://cs.wisc.edu/), advised by [Prof. Rahul Chatterjee](https://pages.cs.wisc.edu/~chatterjee/). I acknowledge the Ho-Chunk Nation on whose ancestral lands I am grateful to work and live as a guest. I deeply respect the knowledge embedded in the Ho-Chunk’s custodianship of Teejop (DeJope) and recognize their continuing connection to land, water, and community here at UW-Madison.


I aim to make technology safe, equitable, and supportive for everyone, especially, communities facing intersecting axes of socio-political violence intersecting race, space and time. My research examines how technologies enables gender-based violence such as intimate partner violence, labor/sex trafficking, and ecological extraction. Specifically, how equitable, scientific design interventions can detect and prevent harm among impacted communities and help them foster collective safety, resistance, and liberation. I combine rigorous data-driven and community-engaged research methods to bridge disciplinary boundaries in and beyond Human-Computer Interaction and Security & Privacy.  I have made contributions across two interwoven threads: (1) designing interventions to support interpersonal violence survivors, and (2) research and design framework to help communities liberate from structural violence. I publish in top-tier venues, such as CHI, CSCW, DIS, USENIX Security, CCS, and, NDSS. I have also presented papers, panels, and, workshops at interdisciplinary venues like [Heidelberg Laureate Forum](https://www.hlf-foundation.org), [Wisconsin State Capitol](https://staterelations.wisc.edu/uw-madison-day-at-the-capitol/#2024-presenters), 
[Cook County Human Trafficking Task Force Conference](https://www.cookcountytaskforce.org/2026-conference.html), 
[Socialism Conference](https://socialismconference.org),
[Gayabii Gawiin](https://lco-nsn.gov/calendar/),
[Women Are Sacred Conference](https://www.niwrc.org/2025-women-are-sacred-conference),
[Reclaiming Our Love Conference](https://the-network.org/reclaimingourlove/), 
and, 
[Native Nations Nursing, Helpers, and Healers Summit](https://nursing.wisc.edu/native-nations-nursing/).

Embracing an advocacy-researcher practice, I direct the operations and outreach at [Madison Tech Clinic (MTC)](https://techclinic.cs.wisc.edu/), a trauma-informed intervention that has supported 180+ survivors with their digital safety needs. I have personally supported 40+ survivors and lead the outreach efforts to build partnerships with 24 GBV advocacy organizations, spreading
MTC’s services across 32 counties in the state of Wisconsin, US. I have been awarded the [Morgridge Fellowship for
Community-Engaged Scholarship](https://morgridge.wisc.edu/2025/08/14/eighth-cohort-of-morgridge-fellows-selected/) to recognize my research practice. And, I have formally trained over 200 health prac-

Center](https://www.niwrc.org/), [Lac Courte Oreilles Tribal Nation](https://www.lac-courte-oreilles-nsn.gov/), state ([EndAbuseWI](https://endabusewi.org/), [The
