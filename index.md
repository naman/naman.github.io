> **_NOTE:_**  I am on the Academic Job Market for postdoc/tenure-track positions starting from 2027 in Information, Computer Science, Communications, and related interdisciplinary fields.


Namaste! I am Naman (he/him), a Computer Science PhD Candidate at the [University of Wisconsin-Madison, USA](https://cs.wisc.edu/), advised by [Prof. Rahul Chatterjee](https://pages.cs.wisc.edu/~chatterjee/). I acknowledge the Ho-Chunk Nation on whose ancestral lands I am grateful to work and live as a guest. I deeply respect the knowledge embedded in the Ho-Chunk’s custodianship of Teejop (DeJope) and recognize their continuing connection to land, water, and community here at UW-Madison.


I aim to make technology safe, equitable, and supportive for everyone, especially, communities facing intersecting axes of socio-political violence intersecting race, space and time. 
My research examines how interpersonal and structural technologies enables online and offline gender-based violence (GBV) such as intimate partner violence, labor/sex trafficking, genocide, and environmental destruction. Specifically, how equitable design interventions (trainings, tools, and advocacy practices) can reduce harm and help marginalized communities foster collective safety, healing, resistance, and liberation.
To do this, I combine ethnographic, participatory, and design research methods to bridge disciplinary boundaries in and beyond human-computer interaction, security & privacy, sociology, psychology, education, politics, law, history, anthropology, & healthcare. 
I have made contributions across two interwoven threads: (1) designing interventions to support interpersonal violence survivors, and (2) research and design framework to help communities liberate from structural violence. I publish in top-tier venues, such as CHI, CSCW,USENIX Security, CCS. I have also presented papers, panels, and, workshops at interdisciplinary venues like 
[Wisconsin State Capitol](https://staterelations.wisc.edu/uw-madison-day-at-the-capitol/#2024-presenters), 
[Cook County Human Trafficking Task Force Conference](https://www.cookcountytaskforce.org/2026-conference.html), 
[Socialism Conference](https://socialismconference.org),
[Gayabii Gawiin](https://lco-nsn.gov/calendar/),
[Women Are Sacred Conference](https://www.niwrc.org/2025-women-are-sacred-conference),
[Reclaiming Our Love Conference](https://the-network.org/reclaimingourlove/), 
and, 
[Native Nations Nursing, Helpers, and Healers Summit](https://nursing.wisc.edu/native-nations-nursing/).

Embracing an advocacy-researcher practice, I direct the operations and outreach at [Madison Tech Clinic (MTC)](https://techclinic.cs.wisc.edu/), a trauma-informed intervention that has supported 180+ survivors (40+ survivors personally) with their digital safety needs. I lead the outreach efforts to build partnerships with [26 urban and rural GBV advocacy organizations](https://techclinic.cs.wisc.edu/partners/) across 32 counties of Wisconsin, US. I have trained over 200 health practitioners, lawyers, judges, and policymakers spanning across the nation ([National Network of Ending Domestic Violence](https://www.nnedv.org/), [National Indigenous Women’s Resource
Center](https://www.niwrc.org/), [Lac Courte Oreilles Tribal Nation](https://www.lac-courte-oreilles-nsn.gov/), state ([EndAbuseWI](https://endabusewi.org/), [The
Network](https://www.the-network.org/), and [Mending the Sacred Hoop](https://mshoop.org)).
I have also been awarded the [Morgridge Fellowship](https://morgridge.wisc.edu/2025/08/14/eighth-cohort-of-morgridge-fellows-selected/) to recognize my community-engaged scholarship.
