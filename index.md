> **_NOTE:_** I am on the Academic Job Market for postdoc/tenure-track positions starting from 2027 in Information, Computer Science, Communications, and related interdisciplinary fields.

Namaste! I am Naman Gupta (he/him), a Computer Science PhD Candidate at the [University of Wisconsin-Madison, USA](https://cs.wisc.edu/), advised by [Prof. Rahul Chatterjee](https://pages.cs.wisc.edu/~chatterjee/). I aim to make technology safe, equitable, and supportive for everyone, especially, communities facing intersecting axes of socio-political violence intersecting race, space, and time.

I am an interdisciplinary Human-AI and Responsible AI researcher who studies how technology and AI
facilitates gender-based violence. I study interpersonal harms (e.g. intimate partner violence, stalking, and
AI-generated intimate imagery) and structural harms (e.g. trafficking, genocide, and environmental violence),
especially in rural and Indigenous communities. To prevent violence and foster safety and sovereignty, I design equitable sociotechnical clinical and health interventions (e.g. education, tools, and advocacy practices) for survivors and frontline providers (health practitioners, legal justice workers, and policymakers).

I combine ethnographic, participatory, and design research methods to study Human-AI interaction and bridge disciplinary boundaries in and beyond computing (human-computer interaction, security & privacy, science & technology studies), sociology (healthcare, politics, law, history, anthropology), and education. In doing so, I have published at top-tier venues, such as CHI (best paper top 1%), CSCW (honorable
mention top 5%), CCS (runners up at CSAW applied research competition), USENIX Security. I have
also disseminated my work publicly at [Wisconsin State Capitol](https://staterelations.wisc.edu/uw-madison-day-at-the-capitol/#2024-presenters), 
[Cook County Human Trafficking Task Force Conference](https://www.cookcountytaskforce.org/2026-conference.html), 
[Socialism Conference](https://socialismconference.org),
[Gayabii Gawiin](https://lco-nsn.gov/calendar/),
[Women Are Sacred Conference](https://www.niwrc.org/2025-women-are-sacred-conference),
[Reclaiming Our Love Conference](https://the-network.org/reclaimingourlove/), 
and, 
[Native Nations Nursing, Helpers, and Healers Summit](https://nursing.wisc.edu/native-nations-nursing/).

I translate research through a community-engaged approach to ensure that the impact is safe, ethical, and grounded in practice. At [Madison Tech Clinic (MTC)](https://techclinic.cs.wisc.edu/), I direct the administration, fundraising, outreach, and have supported 192 survivors (over 40 personally) with their digital safety needs. I have trained over 200 GBV providers at the nation level (National Network of Ending Domestic Violence](https://www.nnedv.org/), National Indigenous Women’s Resource
Center](https://www.niwrc.org/)), Tribal Nation level (Lac Courte Oreilles Tribal Nation](https://www.lac-courte-oreilles-nsn.gov/), [Mending the Sacred Hoop](https://mshoop.org)), and state level ([EndAbuseWI](https://endabusewi.org/), [The Network](https://www.the-network.org/)). I have also designed a hybrid clinical practice for rural survivors to 26 rural GBV organizations across 32 counties of Wisconsin, US. This expansion has supported more than 50 rural survivors so far. I have also been awarded
the [Morgridge Fellowship for Community-engaged Scholarship](https://morgridge.wisc.edu/2025/08/14/eighth-cohort-of-morgridge-fellows-selected/).

I acknowledge the Ho-Chunk Nation on whose ancestral lands I am grateful to work and live as a guest. I deeply respect the knowledge embedded in the Ho-Chunk’s custodianship of Teejop (DeJope) and recognize their continuing connection to land, water, and community here at UW-Madison.
